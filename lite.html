    📏 Calibrate movement
    
      
    Programs and drawn paths turn "go 50 cm" into "drive for 2.5 s". That needs to know how fast your robot moves on your floor.
    
      
    Program speed50%
    
      
    1. Put a ruler next to the robot. Tap Drive 2 s and measure how far it went. Speed = distance ÷ 2.
    
      
    cm per second
    
      
    2. Tap Spin 2 s, estimate how many degrees it turned (one full turn = 360°). Turn speed = degrees ÷ 2.
    
      
    degrees per second
    
      
    Batteries running low make the robot slower, so recalibrate if paths start falling short.
